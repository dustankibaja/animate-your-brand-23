DATA ENGINEERING / INDEPENDENT RESEARCH

Football Intelligence.

A research system for reproducible football probability modeling and evaluation.

The challenge

Build a reproducible pipeline that separates historical data, temporal features, probability generation and evaluation while avoiding unsupported claims about predictive performance.

The engineering

The documented stack includes Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic and PostgreSQL. The research workspace supports manually supplied Poisson scenarios and local assessment inspection. The model pipeline tracks outcome probabilities and research artifacts.

Evidence and limitations

The public README describes versioned models, frozen artifacts, retrospective and prospective evaluation, and a private read-only API. Commercial data and production validation are still gated. This is an independent research project, not a commercial forecasting service or promise of profitable predictions.
