WEB DEVELOPMENT / FOUNDER CONTRIBUTION

Albastini.

A product website for a Tanzanian card game played with physical cards or through its mobile app.

The challenge

Present two ways to play, explain the game, display tournament and winner information, and guide visitors toward the app and physical cards in a consistent brand experience.

The engineering

The project repository documents React 19, TypeScript, TanStack Start server rendering, TanStack Router, Vite and Tailwind. Content is versioned, bilingual English/Kiswahili copy is managed centrally, and tournament states are driven by typed, dated content.

Our founder's role

Website development and brand-related design contributions. This is selected founder experience, not a claim that KB Softwares owns the game or delivered every part of the product.

Evidence and status

The repository includes a final handoff, launch QA and asset provenance documentation. The website is available at its existing project address; current production behavior is subject to separate verification.
