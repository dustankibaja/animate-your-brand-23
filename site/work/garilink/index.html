MOBILE ENGINEERING / FOUNDER CONTRIBUTION

GariLink.

A rental-first vehicle platform with renter, vehicle owner and fleet workflows.

The challenge

Help customers discover suitable vehicles and request rentals while giving vehicle owners a structured way to manage listings and rental operations.

The engineering

The repository documents Flutter application architecture, navigation, authentication, vehicle suitability, availability, rental pricing and booking-request experience, backed by Supabase and PostgreSQL.

Our founder's role

Flutter development, UI/UX and technical delivery contributions. The work illustrates mobile application architecture and business workflow implementation.

Evidence and status

Project documentation includes sprint reports and Android demonstration QA. It also records hosted migration and SQL certification limitations. This case study does not claim production deployment, commercial launch or fully certified backend behavior.
