Is the domain included?

Domain registration, ownership, hosting and renewal costs are agreed in writing before subscribing.

What do the monthly fees cover?

The plan specifies hosting arrangements, included content updates, maintenance, support limits and any separate provider charges.

Can I cancel or move my website?
