What to prepare before building a business website

A successful website project begins with clear business goals and accurate content. Use this checklist before asking for a quote.

Business goals and audience

Define the action you want visitors to take, the customers you serve, and the products or services you want to promote.

Content and identity

Prepare your logo, brand colors, service descriptions, contact details and authentic photographs or project examples. Confirm which information can be published.

Functionality and integrations

List required pages, enquiry methods, payments, bookings, analytics and any content-management needs. Make sure integrations have a real owner and testing plan.

Hosting and search

Choose a public HTTPS URL, set the canonical address, publish a sitemap and ensure important page content is available without relying on client-side rendering. A custom domain can help credibility but is not mandatory for launch.

Launch acceptance

Test mobile layouts, keyboard navigation, links, enquiry delivery, metadata, performance and error pages. Agree on ongoing updates and maintenance.
