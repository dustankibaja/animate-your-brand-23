Privacy information

KB Softwares uses this website to describe services and receive project enquiries. The website's enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser. It does not store submitted details in a website database or send them automatically.

Information you choose to share

If you choose to send a message through WhatsApp, the information you include is shared with WhatsApp and the recipient. Only include information needed for the project discussion. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or sensitive records.

Hosting and third-party services

The hosting provider may process technical request data to operate and secure the website. External links, including WhatsApp and GitHub, are governed by their own privacy practices.

Questions

Contact us through the project contact page with privacy questions or requests.
